Desire: Bouquet Maker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Your bouquets

The bouquets you make and save, including any recipient name you type, are stored only on your device. Desire has no accounts and no servers that receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Usage events

Desire records a small number of simple events, such as a bouquet being generated or the subscription screen being shown, to understand which features are used. In this version those events stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Desire never sees your payment details.

Photos

If you choose to save a florist card, Desire adds that one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Children

Desire is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
